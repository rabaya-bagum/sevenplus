# Beta tester kit

## Message to send testers

> Hi! Thanks for helping test **SevenPlus**, a short-workout app (5–20 minutes, no equipment).
>
> **Install:** get the free **TestFlight** app from the App Store, then open this link on your
> iPhone: [PUBLIC LINK]. Purchases inside the beta are free test purchases, so you won't be charged.
>
> **What I'd love you to do over the next 7 days:**
> 1. Go through setup and do **today's workout** at least 3 times this week.
> 2. Try the **Classic 7-Minute** circuit once (Workouts tab).
> 3. Rate each workout after you finish (Too easy / Just right / Too hard).
> 4. Try one of: a **custom workout**, the **Swap** button during a workout, or **reminders**.
> 5. If you have an Apple Watch, start a workout on the phone and check the watch.
>
> **Something wrong or confusing?** Take a screenshot inside the app and TestFlight will offer
> to send it to me with a note. That's the most useful feedback there is.
>
> I'll send a 2-minute survey at the end of the week. Thank you!

## Beta description (for TestFlight's "What to Test")

> Short no-equipment workouts with a 3D coach. Please do today's workout a few times this week,
> rate each one, and try the Classic 7-Minute circuit. Report anything confusing with a screenshot.

## End-of-week survey (Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or Tally)

1. How many workouts did you do this week? *(0 · 1–2 · 3–4 · 5+)*
2. How easy was setup? *(1 very hard – 5 very easy)*
3. Were the workouts the right difficulty? *(Mostly too easy · Just right · Mostly too hard)*
4. Did the 3D character make the moves clear? *(Always · Mostly · Sometimes · No)* Which move, if any, was unclear?
5. Did anything break, freeze or behave strangely? *(Yes/No, what happened?)*
6. Which feature did you like most? *(Daily workout · Classic circuit · Custom workouts · Swaps · Voice coach · Watch · Reminders · Progress)*
7. What almost made you stop using it?
8. Would you keep using SevenPlus after the beta? *(Definitely · Probably · Not sure · No)*
9. Would you pay for Premium (10–20 min workouts, unlimited custom workouts, adaptive
   difficulty) at about $9.99/month or $59.99/year? *(Yes monthly · Yes yearly · Maybe at a lower price · No)*
10. Anything else?
