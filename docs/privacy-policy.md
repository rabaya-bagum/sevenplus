# SevenPlus Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

SevenPlus is a workout app made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This policy explains what the
app does with your information. The short version: **your workouts stay on your iPhone.** We have
no accounts, no ads, and we don't sell or share your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your device and never sent to us:

- Your profile: goal, fitness level, workout length and any areas you asked to go easy on
- Your workout history, difficulty ratings and daily check-ins (energy and soreness)
- Custom workouts, reminder times and app settings

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also export it or erase it at any time in
**Profile → Your data**.

## Optional features

Each of these is off until you turn it on.

**Apple Health.** If you allow it, SevenPlus saves your finished workouts and estimated active
calories to Apple Health, and reads your body weight to estimate calories. On Apple Watch it
reads your heart rate during a workout to show it on screen. This data stays in Apple Health on
your devices. We never receive it, and it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.
You can change these permissions in the Health app, and **Your data → Erase** can also remove
the workouts SevenPlus saved there.

**Camera rep counting.** If you turn on "Count my reps", the camera is used to detect your body
position and count repetitions. Video is processed on your iPhone in real time. It is never
recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

**Reminders.** Workout reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No data leaves the device.

**Crash reports.** If you turn on **Share crash reports**, then when SevenPlus crashes it sends
a technical report to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry (sentry.io). The report contains where
the app failed, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version. It does not include your
name, workouts, health data or IP address. Reports are kept for up to 30 days and used only to
fix bugs.

**Friend challenges.** If online challenges are available and you create or join one, the
following is stored on our challenge server (hosted by Supabase) so your friends can see the
leaderboard: the name you choose to display, a random ID created by the app (not linked to your
Apple ID, email or phone), and how many workouts you completed during the challenge. Anyone with
the challenge's invite code can see its leaderboard. **Erase all my data** removes you from
every challenge.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells
the app only whether you have an active subscription.

## What we don't do

- No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No selling, renting or sharing of personal information
- No analytics on how you use the app

## Children

SevenPlus is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from
them.

## Your choices and rights

You can export or erase all of your SevenPlus data in **Profile → Your data**, turn optional
features off at any time, and revoke permissions in iOS Settings. Depending on where you live
(for example Canada, the EU or California), you may have further rights to access or delete
information. Contact us for any request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, mention it in
the app's release notes.
